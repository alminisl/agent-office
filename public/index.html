How the office works

Agents

Every Claude Code session from the last 14 days gets a cubicle. Cubicles are grouped by project; the coloured stripe on a nameplate marks the project.

  • Busy: typing at their desk; the bubble shows the current tool
  • Waiting: hand raised, needs your permission or input
  • Your turn: just replied, sitting at their desk waiting for you
  • Idle: you haven't replied for 5 minutes, so they're off on a break (still waiting for you)
  • Offline: out of office 🌴, empty chair (hide them with Show offline)

Progress

Agents earn XP for hands-on work time, tool calls, lines changed and PRs, and level up from Intern to Legend ⭐. They unlock achievements 🏅, and the top earner wears the 👑. The thin bar under each nameplate shows how full their context window is; above 80% they start sweating 🥵.

Promotions

At level 5 an agent moves out of their cubicle into a private office in the executive wing 🎉.

Roles

Hire a PR Reviewer, QA Tester, Bug Hunter, Security Auditor or Docs Reviewer from New session. Let them work inside the office and they run in the background with read-only tools (QA roles may also run tests), then hand in a 📋 report.

Helpers

When a session spawns subagents, little 🤖 helper bots ride up the elevator and stand at the cubicle until their job is done.

Personality

Each personality types, fidgets, talks and takes breaks differently. With Bring this personality to work on, their work style (a Perfectionist tests everything, a Detective finds the root cause first) is added to Claude's system prompt when you start or resume them from the office.

Psst: follow the white rabbit 🐇. And try typing the name of a certain paper company 📎.

On a break they grab coffee, hit the gym, play ping pong or arcade, lounge on the sofa, chat with each other, or peek into a busy colleague's cubicle 👀. Their favourite hangout comes from their personality.

Mouse

  • Click agent or nameplate: copy claude --resume and open their details panel
  • Click empty floor: close the panel

Keyboard

  • ? this help
  • N hire a new agent (new session)
  • D office dashboard (costs, employees, leaderboard, settings)
  • S show / hide offline agents
  • Tab / Shift+Tab cycle through agents
  • O open selected agent in a terminal
  • C copy the resume command
  • A ask the selected agent a question
  • H hide the selected cubicle
  • + / − zoom, 0 fit to window
  • Esc close panel / dialogs

Panel

Work: what they did and said. Ask: questions go to a forked copy of the session, so your real conversation is untouched. Personality: name, character, hangout and look.
